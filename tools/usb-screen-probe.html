USB screen fixed-image probe

Preparation only, not hardware qualification or MarsDisplay integration. No firmware changes.

Windows desktop Edge/Chrome: close vendor apps first. Disconnect other matching devices. Confirm your known SHCHV TUS-35 / USB35INCHIPSV2 in Device Manager, then choose that screen in the browser chooser (currently COM5 for the owner; use your current COM port).

Web Serial cannot expose the USB serial number or COM number to this page. This is your visual verification, not automatic stable identity. VID/PID 1A86:5722 is shared with protocol B devices; matching IDs alone do not establish compatibility. No administrator access or driver installation is required by this probe.

Preview only. Confirm the device before sending.

One send per page load; no automatic retries. After any failed/partial transfer, disconnect/reconnect the device before considering a reload and new explicit send.
